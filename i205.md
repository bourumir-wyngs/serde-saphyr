# serde-saphyr issue #205: resolution review

- **Issue:** [Inconsistent alias errors and diagnostic output (#205)](https://github.com/bourumir-wyngs/serde-saphyr/issues/205)
- **Review date:** 2026-10-09
- **Baseline snapshot:** `master` at [`f1879ab7794b63d710e9dad78975d69c54825ca8`](https://github.com/bourumir-wyngs/serde-saphyr/commit/f1879ab7794b63d710e9dad78975d69c54825ca8). Items 1 and 5a include subsequent local fixes.
